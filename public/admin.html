Recent entries

Upload results spreadsheet

Upload the end-of-day Excel sheet (.xlsx or .xls) or a CSV in the same layout as the printed results: Car #, CC, Class, First Name, LastName, Out?, Year, Make, Model, Color, then RT - 1, ET - 1, MPH - 1, RT - 2… for each day. Drivers are matched by car number. New drivers are added and existing ones updated. Times in the sheet replace the times stored for that car and day. Nothing is deleted, and "Waiver" cells are skipped.
